The Cage

New This Year

  • AI-free, device-free environment

    No personal laptops, phones, AI tools, etc. are all allowed inside the cage.

  • Cage-only challenges

    Some challenges are accessible only from inside the Cage and must be solve there. Challenge files may not leave the Cage.

  • Limited Cage time

    Each team has 2 hours in the Cage, with extra time available only if no other teams are waiting.

  • Don’t break the rules

    We monitor the Cage throughout the event. Any violation by any team member disqualifies the entire team.
